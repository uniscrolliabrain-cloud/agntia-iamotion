# Living procedural design — extensión acumulativa
Conservar P01–44, documentación artística, branding, storyboard y edición asistida como capacidades futuras; prioridad productiva no implica eliminación. Sexta familia: campos de partículas, redes, ondas, geometría procedural y ambientes responsivos. Usos: visuales cognitivos, conexiones de procesos, fondos ambientales discretos, transiciones, presentaciones y feedback. No representar actividad real sin evento host.
ParticleFieldSpec: version,id,seed,domain normalized2d/world3d,emitter shape/rate,capacity,lifetimeMs,initialVelocity,forces[],appearance,interaction,qualityProfiles,fallback. Forces allowlist attract/repel/vortex/drag/noise/flow; finite strength, radius, falloff, maxAcceleration. Simulation fixed timestep, seeded PRNG versioned, bounded pool, stable particle IDs; no unbounded allocations. Offline seek via checkpoints or deterministic replay; live catchup bounded, discard excess elapsed time with diagnostic. Pointer coordinates local normalized, clamp, keyboard equivalent when functional. Resize remaps normalized domain without changing identity; world-space camera reframes separately. Density scales with area under hard cap. Reduced motion static composition; offscreen pause, visibility handling, disposal.
Initial profiles proposed: low 128, medium 512, high 2048 particles; timestep 1/60s, max 4 catchup steps; benchmark before acceptance. SVG for sparse semantic nodes, Canvas2D for moderate fields, WebGL instancing for larger fields; WebGPU future candidate only. No arbitrary shader from LLM. Particle appearance can be points, soft discs, ceramic beads or short trails; no flashing or distracting perpetual high contrast.
