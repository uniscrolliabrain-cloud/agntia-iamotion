Vocabulary: point, line, curve, plane, sphere, ellipsoid, cuboid, cylinder, cone, torus, extrusion, bevel, fillet, chamfer, shell, aperture, seam, joint, hinge, pivot, silhouette, convexity, concavity, symmetry, topology, manifold, normal, UV, occlusion, negative space. GeometrySpec: coordinateConvention, unit, parts[], local transforms, dimensions, primitive, modifiers allowlist, parent, pivot, materialRef, evidence, uncertainty. 2D normalized image coordinates separate from world coordinates; explicit conversion. Part graph acyclic. Single image cannot determine backside; require additional views or label invented reconstruction. Compare silhouette masks, projected landmarks and proportions before texture. Tiny launcher test at 24/32/48/64px; no assumption of validated legibility. Never animate a part unless layer/mesh exists.
