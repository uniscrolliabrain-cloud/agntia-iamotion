# Lighting, color and cinematography
Pixar lighting reference: shading and illumination interact. Taxonomy key/fill/rim/bounce/environment, area/point/directional, soft/hard shadow, contact shadow, specular highlight, diffuse bounce, exposure, white balance, contrast, tone mapping, gamut, display transform. LightSpec type, transform, size, intensity, unit, color or temperature with explicit model. CameraSpec projection, focal length and sensor size or orthographic scale, pose, focus distance, aperture, clipping. Composition terms framing, focal hierarchy, visual weight, balance, negative space, silhouette separation, eye line, foreground/midground/background, depth cues. Motion camera: dolly, pan, tilt, truck, orbit, rack focus; distinguish optical zoom from camera translation. ColorSpec explicit sRGB encoded vs linear; no arithmetic on gamma-encoded colors as light energy. Reference color pipeline for later study ACES/OpenColorIO (not yet inspected). Golden studio scene neutral background, fixed camera, swatch and calibrated exposure; compare renders under same pipeline. No claims of measured physical light from existing raster.
