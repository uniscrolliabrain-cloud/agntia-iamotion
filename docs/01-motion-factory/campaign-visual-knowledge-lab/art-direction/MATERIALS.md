# Material ontology and look development
Inspired by Disney BRDF and OpenPBR. Distinguish material identity, visual intent, estimated appearance and actual shader parameters. MaterialSpec: baseColor {space,values}, metalness [0,1], roughness [0,1], IOR positive, coat weight/roughness, transmission, sheen, subsurface, emission, normal texture ref; optional fields unknown rather than zeros. Renderer mapping versioned; unsupported lobes report degradation. Do not infer PBR values from a single raster. Dielectric vs conductor; ceramic body vs glaze; brushed vs polished metal; paper fiber, wood grain, glass transmission, fabric sheen. Each term has ES/EN label, definition, aliases, counterexamples, compatible geometry and evidence. Agntia ceramic proposal: ivory body, satin glaze, violet enamel accents, champagne hardware; parameters are starting hypotheses not measured. Test material swatches under fixed neutral light, then alternate light, compare silhouette and highlights; human approval across sizes. No proprietary shader copied.
